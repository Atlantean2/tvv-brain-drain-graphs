## 1. `US Talent H-1B registrations and selections FY2021-FY2027.png`
- **Tab:** Q4 US Talent Flows
- **Title:** H-1B lottery entries fell 72% from the FY2024 peak after the $100,000 fee
- **Caption:** H-1B cap registrations: 759k in FY2024, 212k in FY2027 (−38.5% y/y). Source: USCIS, Oct 2026 check.
- **Label:** DATA
- **Bullets:**
  - FY2024: 758,994 eligible registrations, the peak.
  - Sep 19, 2025: a proclamation imposed a $100,000 fee on new H-1B petitions for workers abroad.
  - FY2027: 211,600 properly submitted registrations, down 38.5% from 343,981. It was the first wage-weighted lottery.
  - FY2027 selections: 71.5% held US advanced degrees, up from 57%, and 17.7% were at wage level I.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-1b-specialty-occupations/h-1b-electronic-registration-process
  - https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/alerts/fy-2027-h-1b-initial-registration-selection-process-completed
  - https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/uscis-reaches-fy2027-h1b-visa-cap
- **Doubts:**
  - FY2027 counts "properly submitted" registrations while earlier years count "eligible" ones. USCIS itself compares the two.
  - The FY2024 peak was inflated by duplicate entries. FY2025 moved to unique-beneficiary counting (about 442k beneficiaries in FY25 and about 339k in FY26), so part of the fall predates the fee.
  - The FY2027 selection count was not in the USCIS table, so it is not plotted.

## 2. `US Talent international students enrolled vs OPT 2000-2025.png`
- **Tab:** Q4 US Talent Flows
- **Title:** The US foreign-student record is driven by OPT workers, not enrolment
- **Caption:** 2024/25: 1,177,766 international students; 294,253 on OPT, while enrolled is flat since 2016/17. Source: IIE Open Doors.
- **Label:** DATA
- **Bullets:**
  - 2016/17: 903,127 enrolled, the high point.
  - 2024/25: 883,513 enrolled, 2% below 2016/17.
  - 2024/25: 294,253 on OPT, a record and 25% of the total. Up from 175,695 in 2016/17.
  - The total is 1,177,766, up 4.5% year on year.
- **Sources:**
  - https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Census_Overall-Enrollment-1949-2025_OD25_Website.xlsx
  - https://opendoorsdata.org/annual-release/international-students/
- **Doubts:**
  - 2024/25 predates the 2025 visa pauses. IIE's Fall 2025 snapshot showed new enrolment falling, but it is a survey and is not plotted.
  - OPT counts people working, not studying.

## 3. `US Talent physics PhDs leaving the US AIP 2023-2025.png`
- **Tab:** Q4 US Talent Flows
- **Title:** Foreign physics PhDs leaving the US nearly doubled in one year
- **Caption:** Non-citizen physics PhDs abroad after graduating: 16% (2023-24) to 29% (2024-25). Source: AIP, Aug 4, 2026.
- **Label:** DATA
- **Bullets:**
  - 2023-24 class: about 16% of non-US-citizen physics PhDs lived abroad the winter after graduating.
  - 2024-25 class: about 29%.
  - US citizens: about 5% for the 2022-23 class and almost 12% for 2024-25.
  - Apr 2025: Nature reported US scientists' applications for jobs abroad up 32% in Jan–Mar 2025.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.aip.org/statistics/more-us-trained-physics-phds-are-leaving-the-us
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-025-01216-7
- **Doubts:**
  - These are rounded text values from AIP's write-up, not microdata, and the base years for the two groups differ as AIP published them.
  - Physics only.
  - The Nature figure (a single job board's data) is a bullet, not plotted.

## 4. `Canada non-permanent residents inflows vs outflows 2021-2026.png`
- **Tab:** Q1 (Canada)
- **Title:** Temporary residents now leave Canada faster than they arrive
- **Caption:** Canada's non-permanent residents over the last four quarters: 441k arrived, 596k left (to Q2 2026). Source: Statistics Canada 17-10-0040.
- **Label:** DATA
- **Bullets:**
  - Q3 2023: inflows peaked at 430,470.
  - Q4 2024: outflows overtook inflows (179,998 vs 148,309), and they have exceeded them in 5 of the 7 quarters since.
  - Q4 2025: inflows were 76,983, the lowest in the series, against outflows of 156,781.
  - Q3 2025–Q2 2026: a net loss of about 155k temporary residents.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1710004001
- **Doubts:**
  - The data are preliminary and were downloaded Oct 25, 2026; a TLS error blocked a refresh on Oct 30.
  - Outflows include people moving to permanent residence status, so they are not all departures. StatCan's NPR "outflows" combine transitions to permanent residence with emigration.

## 5. `Europe young citizens aged 25-34 emigrating Italy Spain Germany Romania 2013-2024.png`
- **Tab:** Q2 (Europe)
- **Title:** Italy's young emigrants hit a record 55,700, double 2013
- **Caption:** Italian citizens aged 25–34 emigrating: 55,724 in 2024, up from 26,414 in 2013. Source: Eurostat migr_emi1ctz.
- **Label:** DATA
- **Bullets:**
  - 2013: 26,414 Italians aged 25–34 emigrated.
  - 2023: 42,216.
  - 2024: 55,724, up 32% year on year and the series high.
  - 2024 for the others: Germany 36,492 (estimate), Romania 32,469 (estimate), Spain 28,257.
- **Sources:**
  - https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/migr_emi1ctz/default/table
- **Doubts:**
  - Germany's 2016 and Spain's 2021 values carry Eurostat break flags.
  - The German and recent Romanian values are flagged as estimates.
  - Records of departures under-count emigrants who don't deregister.
  - This complements, and doesn't duplicate, the existing chart of nationals of all ages.
